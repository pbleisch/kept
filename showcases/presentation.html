The Three Properties

Every artifact has content. It may have standing, and it may have truth. None of the three converts into another.

Content

Authored by a person. The statement, the predicate, the scope, the prose. Free to write, free to change. Grants nothing.

Standing

Created only by ratify — a human act. Passed to work by delegation. Answers: may this oblige anyone?

Truth

Acquired only by observing the world. Derived when read. Answers: is this so?

drift = artifacts where standing ≠ none
              and truth       ≠ kept

A promise with standing and without truth is drift. Work is what you do about it.

The Nouns

One artifact type — artifact — differentiated by what it does and what kept knows about it.

The big three — artifacts that carry standing

These are the only artifacts that can be ratified. They create binding force.

Promise

A commitment to keep something true. Checked every tick, drift reported, repair dispatchable.

Policy

A rule that must hold before a change lands. Blocks bad changes during a land's gate.

Decision

A ratified explanation of why something was done. Tracks its revision — change it, dependent promises drop standing.

The remaining types — valuable because of what kept knows

Document

An explanation or argument. Purely informational. Links resolved, broken references reported. Citable by other artifacts.

Fact

A claim about what the world was like. Carries a modality — how well-established the claim is. Used to record observations with confidence levels, without committing to checking them again.

Desire

A want with no standing. Four derived states — none stored, computed on read. Committed when a ratified artifact realizes it.

3 / 9

The Loop

kept ticks once and exits. No scheduler. No daemon. A person runs it the way they run make.

Tick

Automatic. Gathers, evaluates, records truth. Never dispatches. Opens a thread but waits for a person to choose which drift to repair.

Repair

Human-initiated. Cuts a box, measures the gap, dispatches a confined agent, re-derives from the tree the session left. Nothing lands. The box is discarded.

Landing

Human-initiated. The only verb that reaches the network. Gates outside a lock, checks whether the target moved, then merges and writes — ref advance or working-tree arrival.

the ledger — append-only, on its own ref standing, truth, observations, dispositions, threads No. Nothing re-derives an act.
derived — caches and boxes thrown away every tick Yes. gitignored.

Imagine a rulebook that lives in a room with no doors. The agent is allowed inside the house to rearrange furniture (write files), but the rulebook lives on a shelf in a room that does not exist inside the house at all. The agent literally cannot reach it — not because it is forbidden, but because it is nowhere in the house.

That is the most important boundary in the system: the agent writes content but can never read or write the record of what it is allowed to do.

Confinement

A repair box is a worktree. That confines nothing by itself — a process inside one can still read every credential on the machine, reach any host, and push to origin.

Scope

What may land. Out-of-scope paths are reported, never landed. A glob rendered into filesystem grants.

Box

What reaches the repository. A worktree and a gate that re-derives from the world.

Confinement

What the process may touch. Writable paths, reachable hosts, credentials held, programs runnable. Enforced by nono.

A canary attempts ten escapes before any session starts — each an attempt that must come back refused. A probe that could not be attempted counts as a failure. The box is not the jail. What the jail refused is not reported at all.

The Desire Pipeline

A desire is a want with no standing and no truth. Nobody ratifies it. Nothing observes it. That is what makes the pipeline safe: an agent can write a hundred candidates and nothing changes.

Four states — derived, not stored

No status: field. Whether a desire is raw, shaped, committed, or retired is computed on read from what it says, from ratified artifacts that realize it, and from the acts naming it.

The trial is the teeth

kept shape --trial runs a candidate promise against the current world and prints what it would say. A candidate already reading kept must be justified before ratification — catches vacuity before standing is granted.

What Is Next

agent — unwritten

A general dispatch verb. The tick opens threads but never dispatches. agent would let a person (or a policy with a deadline) dispatch any pending thread. A ranking and budget are needed first.

promote — unwritten

Move a fact to a wider scope. Still a fact, still no standing. A fact observed in a narrow box could be promoted to a broader context — a measurement that holds everywhere, not just in one thread.

Self-Dispatch

When a desire’s ripe_when fires, the system could auto-shape it: dispatch a shaping session to write a candidate promise, then auto-dispatch a repair if the trial reads not-kept. The pipeline would close itself.

Summary

What it is

A system for recording commitments, observing whether they still hold, and closing the gap when they don't.

Key ideas

Three independent properties — content, standing, truth. Standing is created only by ratification. Truth only by observation. Neither converts into the other.

The ledger lives on a git ref no box checks out. The repair agent writes content but never holds the bytes that say what it is allowed to do.

Where it came from

A synthesis of nine predecessor systems, each evaluated under real use. The parts that survived — the corpus, the thread loop, the box, the sandbox — assembled into one coherent design. 86 decisions, 86 rejected alternatives documented.
